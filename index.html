About Rivana

We combine maritime know-how with advanced technology to transform day-to-day marine operations into smarter, safer, and more efficient workflows.

Vision

To become the leading provider of innovative maritime technology solutions in the Kingdom and globally.

Mission

To deliver integrated, technology-driven maritime services that enhance safety, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

Our Values

  • Innovation — We use technology to unlock smarter maritime operations.
  • Excellence — We pursue high standards in quality, performance, and delivery.
  • Safety — People, customers, and the marine environment are at the core of every operation.

What we do

  • End-to-end digital solutions for fleets, marinas, and marine businesses.
  • Real-time monitoring, automation, and analytics dashboards.
  • Operational visibility and better decision-making through data.

Our Services

From sea to system — comprehensive services that merge maritime expertise with advanced technology.

Smart Fleet Management System

  • Real-time monitoring of vessels and assets to improve operational efficiency.
  • Fuel consumption tracking and preventive maintenance scheduling to reduce costs.
  • Safety alerts for speed, routes, and anomalies.
  • Remote control & monitoring via digital systems (e.g., SCADA) and live dashboards.
  • AI-assisted analytics and recommendations to optimize performance.
  • Compliance support aligned with local and international quality & safety requirements.

Smart Marina Management

  • Live berth availability and occupancy tracking to reduce congestion.
  • Automated onboarding, e-permits, and digital payments.
  • Utilities & resource monitoring per berth (water, electricity, fuel).
  • User apps for reservations, payments, alerts, and service requests.
  • Environmental capabilities (waste management insights & water-quality monitoring).
  • Security: sensors, CCTV integration, GPS tracking, and centralized control.

SCADA Integration & IoT

  • Real-time data collection from sensors and field equipment.
  • Remote operations: start/stop systems from anywhere via cloud interfaces.
  • Lower field-visit needs and faster incident response.
  • Energy, water, and fuel optimization to reduce waste.
  • Early-warning safety systems (leak, overheating, power loss).
  • Integration-ready with ERP/CRM and unified dashboards.

Marine CRM / ERP

  • Unified platform connecting procurement, ops, maintenance, finance, HR, and fleet data.
  • Client journey tracking: bookings, invoices, preferences, service history, satisfaction.
  • Automation to reduce manual work and improve data accuracy.
  • Preventive maintenance tracking and spare-parts inventory management.
  • Financial transparency: real-time reports, budgets, e-payments, and bank integrations.
